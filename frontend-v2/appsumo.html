Common questions

Objection scaffold for real buyer conversations.

Short answers to the questions that matter before a lifetime purchase.

Can I use the product for ETFs and mutual funds?

Yes, supported funds are handled with labelled fund data such as holdings, costs, allocation, performance and risk. Company SEC filing metrics are not silently applied to funds.

What if a filing is missing or the AI is uncertain?

The product says when a value or period is unavailable and links the underlying source where one is available. It is a research aid, not a promise of complete coverage or investment advice.

What does lifetime access actually include?

Your AppSumo tier controls the listed features and monthly Ask allowance. The live AppSumo listing and your account are the authority for current redemption, refund and entitlement terms.

Questions before buying

Read the limits as carefully as the features.

Where does an answer get its information?

Reported company fundamentals are derived from supported SEC filings and keep their fiscal context. Fund questions use labelled fund-market data. Information after the latest filing can use dated live-web sources. The answer should identify which source class it used.

Can the AI still make a mistake?

Yes. No AI or extraction pipeline is infallible. The product is designed to retrieve evidence, show its source and avoid answering from model memory, but you should still inspect the cited filing or source before making a material decision.

What changes between Starter, Investor and Pro?

Every tier builds Research Dossiers. The Monitor watchlist scales with the tier — 1, 4 or 8 companies — and so does the monthly AI credit allowance: 50, 150 or 400. A Dossier costs 10 credits, a Filing Monitor report 5, a comparison 5, a follow-up question 2. AppSumo’s live listing is the final authority on what each tier includes.

What is a Research Dossier?

An initiation-grade report on a covered US stock — decision brief, filed trajectory, segments, valuation, bull and bear case, risks and the latest filing change — written without prompting and with every figure cited to the filing it came from. Because every dossier follows the same structure, companies can be compared side by side.

Is AppSumo Pro the same as the website Pro plan?

No. AppSumo tiers are one-time lifetime-deal entitlements; the website plans are recurring subscriptions with separate limits and billing. Redeeming an AppSumo license links its applicable lifetime entitlement to your StockPortfolio.pro account. The live AppSumo listing and your account screen are the authority for the terms that apply.

Does the deal include ETFs and mutual funds?

Yes, supported ETFs and mutual funds are available in the research workspace. Fund answers use labelled fund data—holdings, costs, allocations, performance and risk—not company SEC 10-K/10-Q figures.

What happens when new features launch?

Lifetime access remains governed by your AppSumo tier and its redemption terms. New features may have different eligibility or usage limits, which we will show in the product instead of promising unlimited access to every future feature.

Do I need to connect a brokerage account?

No broker credentials are required. You can add holdings manually. If automatic brokerage aggregation is essential to your workflow, this is not the right product today.

Does “lifetime” mean unlimited AI usage?

No. It means lifetime access under the applicable AppSumo deal terms; Ask has a monthly allowance based on the tier. The usage limits make the AI component explicit rather than describing a metered service as unlimited.

Can I publish or use the output for clients?

The product includes tools for preparing research to share. Public sharing and commercial or client-facing licensing are not necessarily the same right. Review the current AppSumo terms and contact support if your use is paid, regulated or client-facing.

Is this investment advice?

No. StockPortfolio.pro does not execute trades or provide personalized recommendations. It surfaces research evidence; you remain responsible for verification and decisions.
