Customer ambassador program

Plain-language ambassador terms

These terms govern StockPortfolio.pro's private Release 1 customer ambassador program. They do not change your product plan, AppSumo entitlement, website subscription, AI limit or customer support.

1. Who may participate

  • Release 1 is private and invite only. There is no open enrollment or public application.
  • You must be an existing, reconciled paying StockPortfolio.pro customer who has achieved a successful research outcome.
  • An invitation is personal to the account that received it. It cannot be sold, transferred or shared with another promoter.
  • Your referral link remains inactive until you sign in and explicitly accept the current version of these terms.
  • An invitation, activation, commission approval or payout is never automatic.

2. Acceptance and later versions

  • Accepting the dashboard checkbox records your acceptance of version customer-ambassador-v1-2026-08-13 (dated 2026-08-13).
  • If these terms materially change, the dashboard may require you to review and accept a later identified version before sharing again.
  • Continuing to use an active referral link means you will follow the version you accepted and applicable law.

3. Your relationship and required disclosure

  • You are an independent customer ambassador, not an employee, adviser, representative or agent of StockPortfolio.pro.
  • You must make the commission relationship obvious wherever you endorse or recommend StockPortfolio.pro.
  • Place the disclosure with the endorsement itself, in the same language, where a reader will see it without opening a profile page or searching through hashtags.
  • Do not remove or obscure the disclosure from the default share message.
Affiliate link — I may earn a commission if you purchase, at no extra cost to you. StockPortfolio.pro is research software, not personal investment advice.

4. Eligible referral attribution

  • A valid referral link records a first-party click and sets a signed cookie for 60 days.
  • The last eligible ambassador click during that window receives attribution. A later eligible ambassador click replaces an earlier one.
  • Direct, organic and internal visits do not erase an otherwise eligible ambassador click.
  • Only a genuinely new customer can create commission. Your own purchases, existing-customer purchases and manipulated attribution are ineligible.
  • The cookie contains an opaque ambassador slug, random click ID and timestamp—not an email, customer identity, password, license key or payment data.

5. Commission rates and eligible basis

  • Stripe plans through Power: 30% of eligible collected first-year revenue. A monthly subscription can generate commission on no more than its first 12 paid invoices. An annual subscription can generate commission only on its first eligible paid invoice.
  • Stripe Desk: 20% under the same eligible-invoice limits.
  • Stripe Firm/Enterprise: 10% under the same eligible-invoice limits.
  • AppSumo: 25% of actual net partner proceeds after discounts, fees, credits and refunds—not 25% of the displayed customer price. The reconciled AppSumo Partner Portal export is authoritative.
  • Taxes, refunded amounts, chargebacks and amounts that StockPortfolio.pro did not collect do not increase the eligible basis.

6. Holds, reversals and disputes

  • Stripe commission has a 30-day refund and dispute hold.
  • AppSumo commission has a 60-day hold and must be reconciled against actual partner proceeds.
  • A refund, chargeback, dispute, deactivated license, duplicate event, self-referral or other ineligible transaction can suspend or reverse the related commission.
  • "Pending" means recorded but not payable. It may still be inside a hold or waiting for administrator review.
  • "Approved" means reviewed and eligible for a future manual payout batch, subject to the threshold and any later valid reversal.

7. Manual payouts, threshold and tax

  • The minimum payout is 100.00 per ambassador in one currency of approved, unreversed commission. Balances from different ambassadors or currencies are not combined to reach the threshold.
  • Release 1 has no automatic payout provider. An administrator reviews eligible commission, prepares a batch, pays it externally and then records the payment reference.
  • A hold-until or estimated review date is not a guaranteed payment date.
  • You must provide accurate payout details through the approved private process when requested. Never send banking credentials through a public form or referral link.
  • You are responsible for reporting and paying taxes that apply to your commission. StockPortfolio.pro may require appropriate tax or identity documentation before payment where required.

8. Honest reviews are separate from commission

  • Commission is earned only from an eligible attributed purchase under these terms.
  • Commission is never offered for writing, changing or removing a review.
  • If you choose to review StockPortfolio.pro, it must reflect your honest experience and comply with the review platform's rules.
  • Private support, onboarding help and an existing public review do not create referral or commission eligibility.

9. Financial research and promotional claims

  • StockPortfolio.pro provides informational research software. It does not provide personal financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
  • Do not promise investment returns, outperformance, loss prevention, data perfection, uptime or business longevity.
  • Do not tell anyone to buy, sell or hold a security on StockPortfolio.pro's behalf.
  • AI output can contain mistakes. Check it against the cited filing or primary source before presenting it as fact.
  • Describe limitations fairly. Do not omit a material limitation to make a promotion more persuasive.

10. Prohibited promotion

  • No spam, automated unsolicited messages, deceptive redirects or purchased traffic presented as genuine interest.
  • No self-referrals, duplicate ambassador identities, cookie stuffing, attribution tampering or attempts to claim an existing customer.
  • No impersonating StockPortfolio.pro, confusing domains, unauthorized brand-search ads or false claims of employment or endorsement.
  • No unauthorized discounts, cashback, rebates, bonuses or other financial incentives.
  • No publishing customer identities, private research, portfolios, license codes, payment information or confidential support conversations.
  • No unlawful, misleading, abusive or discriminatory content.

11. Suspension and closure

  • StockPortfolio.pro may pause a link or decline an invitation while investigating fraud, complaints, eligibility, misleading claims or a breach of these terms.
  • A suspended or declined ambassador cannot create new eligible referrals. Historical privacy-safe records may remain available for reconciliation.
  • Valid approved commission earned before closure remains subject to holds, reversals, the payout threshold and manual review.

12. Questions and support

Ask before sharing if a promotion, disclosure or commission rule is unclear. Contact support@stockportfolio.pro. The main terms of use and privacy policy also continue to apply.
