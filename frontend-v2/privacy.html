Privacy & data handling

Privacy policy

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have. The product is designed to use the minimum data needed to deliver account access, research tools, and support.

1. Data we collect

  • Account information you provide: name, email address, and a password hash.
  • Portfolio data you choose to store, such as holdings and purchase details.
  • Watchlists and saved screens you create.
  • Questions you put to Ask and its answers, kept briefly so conversations can continue across sessions, plus optional thumbs-up/down feedback used to improve answer quality.
  • Support submissions you send through the support form.
  • Technical logs required for security, debugging, and uptime monitoring.
  • If you follow an approved ambassador link, an opaque referral slug, random click identifier, click time, landing path and limited fraud-prevention signals. The referral record does not contain the ambassador's or visitor's email address.

2. How we use data

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Provide the screener, company pages, markets, Ask, and portfolio features.
  • Respond to support requests and service enquiries.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and misuse of the platform.
  • Attribute an eligible new-customer purchase to an approved customer ambassador, reconcile refunds, and maintain an auditable commission ledger.

3. Analytics, cookies, and consent

  • Analytics (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) load only after you opt in via the consent banner — if you decline, the scripts are never loaded at all.
  • Measurement is used for conversion and product-usage understanding only.
  • Ad personalisation is never enabled.
  • Browser storage retains your session token, preferences, and your consent choice.
  • An approved ambassador link may set a signed, first-party referral cookie for up to 60 days. It contains only an opaque ambassador slug, random click identifier and timestamp; it does not contain a name, email address, password, licence code, portfolio or payment information.

4. Data retention and security

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • Affiliate click, order and commission records may be retained beyond the cookie's 60-day lifetime where needed for attribution, refund reconciliation, fraud prevention, tax, accounting or dispute handling.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.
  • We use technical and organisational measures to protect stored data.
  • No system is perfectly secure; we continuously improve safeguards.

5. Sharing and processors

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Service providers may process limited data to operate infrastructure, AI answering, and billing (payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number).
  • Ambassadors see aggregate clicks and privacy-safe order and commission states. They do not receive a referred customer's name, email, research questions, portfolio, licence code or payment details.
  • Where required, processors are bound by contractual data-protection obligations.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, and objection rights.
  • Data portability where applicable under local law.
  • You may withdraw optional analytics consent at any time (clear the choice in your browser storage, or email us).

7. Contact and policy updates

For privacy requests, email support@stockportfolio.pro. We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes; material updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Using stockportfolio.pro means you acknowledge this privacy policy and our terms of use.
