Starting brief example · sources checked September 29, 2026

Three companies. The facts to keep separate.

This illustrates the starting brief using the June 2026 reporting periods below. It is not a claim that these filings appeared this week, nor a complete review of subsequent filings. Your Friday email covers an explicitly dated review window.

Apple · AAPL

Form 10-Q · quarter ended June 27, 2026 · MD&A, pages 15–16

Reported: Services sales were $30.739 billion, up from $27.423 billion in the comparable quarter. Services gross margin was 75.6% in both periods.

What that separates: Services revenue grew while its reported gross margin percentage stayed flat. Growth in sales and improvement in margin are different claims.

Question for the next filing: Does Services revenue continue to grow, and does its gross margin percentage change?

Read Apple’s filing — Products and Services Performance; Gross Margin

FormFactor · FORM

Form 10-Q · quarter ended June 27, 2026 · income statement

Reported: Quarterly revenue was $258.242 million versus $195.798 million a year earlier. Operating income was $57.799 million versus $12.306 million.

Calculated: Operating income divided by revenue gives an operating margin of approximately 22.4%, versus 6.3%. The source table reports dollars in thousands; the amounts above are converted to millions.

Question for the next review: Which product mix and cost changes explain the margin movement? These totals alone do not establish the cause.

Read FormFactor’s filed income statement

SharonAI · SHAZ

Form 10-Q · quarter ended June 30, 2026 · Note 20, Commitments

Disclosed: The company describes an ESDS services arrangement of $1.26 billion and approximately $720 million of expected equipment and infrastructure capital expenditure to support it. Procurement had begun by June 30, with some payments recorded as prepayments.

What remains unanswered: This commitment disclosure does not establish later delivery, customer acceptance, or collection of the full contract value. Those milestones need their own dated evidence.

Read SharonAI’s filing — Note 20, Hardware Procurement
