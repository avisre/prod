Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the controls you have when using stockportfolio.pro. We design the product to use the minimum data needed to deliver account access, portfolio tools, and support.

Guarded Data

Built for secure portfolio tracking

We keep collection narrow and focused on essential product operation.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information you provide: name, email address, and password hash.
  • Portfolio data you choose to store, such as holdings and purchase details.
  • Support submissions you send through the support form.
  • Technical logs required for security, debugging, and uptime monitoring.

2. How We Use Data

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Provide dashboard, fundamentals, and news functionality.
  • Respond to support requests and service inquiries.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and misuse of the platform.

3. Analytics, Cookies, and Consent

  • Google Analytics is loaded with consent defaults set to denied.
  • Analytics measurement is enabled only after user consent.
  • Ad personalization is not enabled in this consent flow.
  • Browser storage may retain preferences and attribution parameters.

4. Data Retention and Security

  • Account data is retained while your account is active.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • We use technical and organizational measures to protect stored data.
  • No system is perfectly secure; we continuously improve safeguards.

5. Sharing and Processors

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Service providers may process limited data to operate infrastructure and billing.
  • Where required, processors are bound by contractual data-protection obligations.

6. Your Rights

  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, and objection rights.
  • Data portability where applicable under local law.
  • You may withdraw optional analytics consent at any time.

7. Contact and Policy Updates

For privacy requests, email support@stockportfolio.pro.

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
