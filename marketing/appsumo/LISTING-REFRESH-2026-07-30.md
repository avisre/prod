# AppSumo listing conversion refresh

This is the source-of-truth brief for the next Partner Portal listing edit. It
does not claim that a portal edit has happened until the live listing is checked.

## Above the fold

**For:** self-directed US-stock investors and analysts who want to verify a
company claim against the filed period and source.

**Outcome:** go from ticker/question to a reproducible filing-backed answer,
comparison, screen, or portfolio check without manually stitching together SEC
documents and spreadsheets.

**Boundary:** StockPortfolio.pro is an informational research workflow—not a
stock-pick service, price-prediction engine, or guarantee that AI is infallible.

## Three workflows to demonstrate

1. Check whether net income is supported by operating cash flow, with the
   annual period and SEC source visible.
2. Compare filed share counts while flagging split-sized or non-comparable
   periods rather than publishing a misleading dilution percentage.
3. Ask a company question, inspect the cited filing context, and continue into
   the deterministic company/filing view.

## 60–90 second demo outline

- 0–10s: state the period/source problem.
- 10–30s: run the free earnings-quality or dilution checker.
- 30–55s: open the cited source and show the reporting period.
- 55–75s: continue into the full workflow/Ask view.
- 75–90s: show AppSumo tiers and the important usage limits without hiding
  them.

## Required screenshots

- Tool input and complete result with ticker, period, units, and SEC source.
- Ask answer with source context open.
- Comparison or portfolio workflow with a real research outcome.
- AppSumo activation/onboarding step.

## Objections the listing must answer

- Difference from free quote/fundamental sites.
- Which figures come from SEC filings and which sources cover funds/markets.
- Data refresh timing and known limitations.
- AI/Ask usage limits by lifetime tier.
- What happens when data is missing or periods are not comparable.
- How redemption and support through support@stockportfolio.pro work.

Purchase counts must be reconciled against the AppSumo Partner Portal. Reviews
may be requested only honestly after meaningful product use, with no incentive.
