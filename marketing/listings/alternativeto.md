## Long description
stockportfolio.pro is a research tool for long-term investors who want answers they can trust. Its "Ask AI" analyst answers questions about any US-listed company **only from official SEC filings (10-K/10-Q via EDGAR), never from memory** — every figure shows its source, with charts and tables instead of vague prose.

Beyond Ask, it's a calm, single-screen workflow: a fundamentals database computed deterministically from filings (up to 19 years of history, split-adjusted), a screener over the US universe, side-by-side company comparisons with an AI verdict, guru-portfolio tracking, a Filing Monitor that watches for new SEC filings, a research dossier, and a personal portfolio tracker. Coverage spans 1,500+ S&P 500 / MidCap 400 / SmallCap 600 names plus a directory of ~10,000 SEC registrants.

The pitch versus a general chatbot: a general LLM will confidently invent a number. stockportfolio.pro won't — answers are pinned to the filing line item, so you can act on them.

## Key features
- Ask AI — company Q&A grounded only in SEC filings, with a source under every figure
- Fundamentals from filings: revenue, net income, margins, P/E, ROE, dividends, FCF — up to 19 years
- Stock screener over the US universe
- Side-by-side stock comparison + AI verdict
- Guru portfolios (track notable investors' holdings)
- Filing Monitor — alerts on new SEC filings
