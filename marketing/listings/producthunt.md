## Description (≤ 260 chars)
Ask anything about a US stock and get an answer pinned to its SEC 10-K/10-Q filings — with the source under every figure. Plus filings-derived fundamentals (19 yrs), a screener, side-by-side comparisons, guru portfolios and a portfolio tracker. Free to try.

## First comment (maker's story)
Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built stockportfolio.pro because I kept asking ChatGPT about stocks and getting numbers that were confidently wrong. For investing, a made-up margin or revenue figure isn't a quirk — it's a way to lose money.

So this analyst does the opposite: it answers **only from official SEC filings (10-K/10-Q via EDGAR), never from memory**, and puts the source under every figure. Ask "how has NVIDIA's gross margin moved over 12 quarters?" and you get a chart, a table, and the filing it came from.

Around that, it's a calm, single-screen research workflow:
- 📊 Fundamentals from filings — up to 19 years, split-adjusted
- 🔎 Screener over the US universe
- ⚖️ Side-by-side comparison + an AI verdict on the stronger business
- 🧭 Guru portfolios, Filing Monitor (new-filing alerts), research dossier
- 💼 Portfolio tracker — no broker connection required

Free tier to try it, 7-day Pro trial with no card. I'd love your feedback — especially on the Ask answers. Tell me where it's still not nuanced enough.
