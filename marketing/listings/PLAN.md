## Sequencing notes
- **AlternativeTo + SaaSHub + Slant + StackShare** are the quick wins — knock them
  out in one sitting; each is a 5-minute form using [directories.md](directories.md).
- **Product Hunt** has no instant launch — schedule for a Tue–Thu 12:01am PT and
  line up ~10 supporters first. Don't burn it on a random weekday.
- **Hacker News** is high-variance: post **Show HN** in the morning ET on a weekday,
  reply fast and technically. One good thread can outweigh everything else here.
- **Reddit** removes overt promo. Lead with a genuinely useful, filing-grounded
  analysis (your @avisre voice) and mention the tool once, softly. See the asset.
- **G2/Capterra** matter most once you have a handful of happy users who'll leave
  **real** reviews — don't fabricate reviews; it's against their TOS and obvious.
