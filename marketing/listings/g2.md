## What is stockportfolio.pro? (overview)
stockportfolio.pro is a web-based stock-research platform for self-directed investors and finance professionals. Its defining feature is an AI analyst that answers questions about any US-listed company **strictly from official SEC filings (10-K/10-Q)** — never from model memory — and shows the source under every figure. Around that sit a filings-derived fundamentals database (up to 19 years of history), a screener, side-by-side comparisons, guru-portfolio tracking, a Filing Monitor and a portfolio tracker.

## Key benefits
1. **Trustworthy answers.** Responses are pinned to filing line items, so you get auditable numbers, not hallucinations — the core gap with general chatbots for finance.
2. **Depth without spreadsheets.** Up to 19 years of revenue, margins, P/E, ROE, FCF and dividends, computed deterministically from filings and split-adjusted.
3. **Decisions, faster.** Screen the US universe, compare two companies side by side with an AI verdict, and track a portfolio — in one calm, single-screen workflow.
4. **Stay current.** Filing Monitor surfaces new SEC filings as they land.
