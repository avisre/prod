## One-line positioning
An AI equity-research analyst grounded only in SEC filings — auditable answers, with the filing source under every figure — for advisors who can't afford a hallucinated number in a client conversation.

## Why it fits an advisor's stack
- **Auditable, not generative-guesswork.** Every answer is pinned to a 10-K/10-Q line item. An advisor can cite the filing, not "the AI said so" — which matters for compliance and for client trust on a YMYL topic.
- **Filing Monitor.** Watch holdings and watchlist names for new SEC filings, so a material 8-K/10-Q doesn't get missed before a review meeting.
- **Research dossier + comparisons.** Pull a defensible one-screen brief on a company, or a side-by-side of two with an AI verdict, ahead of an IPS discussion.
- **Deterministic fundamentals.** Up to 19 years of filings-derived figures, split-adjusted — consistent across companies, not scraped estimates.
