# AppSumo sprint runbook

1. Confirm the live listing, tiers, expiration, refund terms, and Partner Portal numbers.
2. Keep `AFFILIATE_PROGRAM_ENABLED` false.
3. Contact existing buyers with support first: one ticker/question, one cited result, then ask whether it helped.
4. Request an honest review only after the review-eligibility rule is met; never ask for a 5-star rating or offer an incentive.
5. Reconcile the Partner Portal CSV manually before entering GMV snapshots.
6. Enable the affiliate pilot only after three real customers choose “Yes, clearly” and the feature is deliberately enabled by the owner.
