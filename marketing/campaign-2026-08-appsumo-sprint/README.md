# AppSumo August 2026 sprint

This is the operating folder for the revenue-focused pilot. AppSumo is the primary checkout; website, social, creator, and community traffic retain first/last-touch source and content IDs. The tracker starts blank so it cannot imply reach, revenue, or customers that have not been verified.

The target is $100,000 in net revenue, measured against Partner Portal evidence. The dashboard at `/admin/growth/august-2026` is private to the existing marketing-dashboard account and accepts manual GMV snapshots without making purchases or payouts.

## Assets and reporting

- `assets/appsumo-sales-demo.mp4` is a 60–100 second public-site walkthrough.
- `assets/appsumo-onboarding.mp4` is a 2–3 minute public-site onboarding walkthrough.
- The same approved videos are served from `/assets/appsumo-sales-demo.mp4` and `/assets/appsumo-onboarding.mp4` after deployment.
- `x-opportunities-2026-08-09.csv` contains 20 researched, tracked reply opportunities; the 20 replies and two CTA replies were published and verified on 2026-08-09. `x-original-posts-2026-08-09.md` records the two published originals and their public URLs.
- `creators.csv` contains 31 public creator or publication prospects with individualized angles. Nine verified, high-fit contacts were emailed individually from `support@stockportfolio.pro` on 2026-08-09; the remaining rows are research candidates, not evidence of outreach or endorsement.
- `daily/2026-08-09.md` is generated by `scripts/august-daily-growth-report.js` and keeps Partner Portal GMV separate from any list-price proxy.
