## Approved copy

### Reddit — answer first, link second

> A quick way to sanity-check an earnings headline is to compare net income with operating cash flow for the same annual reporting period, then disclose capex and the resulting free cash flow. Period mismatches and missing values matter, so I would not reduce this to a single “quality score.” I built a small deterministic checker that shows the reported periods, values and SEC source links: [earnings quality checker](https://www.stockportfolio.pro/tools/earnings-quality?source=reddit&content_id=tool-earnings-quality&click_id=community-reddit-20260809). It is informational, not investment advice.

### Hacker News — Show HN draft

> Show HN: StockPortfolio.pro’s free deterministic filing tools. Enter a ticker to inspect earnings quality, share-count changes or a recent SEC filing timeline. The tools reuse filed data, show the refresh date and link back to SEC sources; they do not call AI or generate predictions. Feedback on missing data and workflow clarity is welcome: [filing timeline](https://www.stockportfolio.pro/tools/filing-timeline?source=hn&content_id=tool-filing-timeline&click_id=community-hn-20260809).
