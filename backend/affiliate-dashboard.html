Release 1 rules

The terms behind the numbers

Attribution

The last eligible ambassador click within 60 days wins. A later eligible ambassador click can replace an earlier one; direct, organic and internal visits do not erase it. New customers only—self-referrals and existing-customer purchases are ineligible.

Stripe commission

30% through Power, 20% for Desk and 10% for Firm/Enterprise. Monthly commission stops after 12 paid invoices; annual commission applies once, to the first eligible paid invoice.

AppSumo commission

25% of actual net partner proceeds after discounts, fees, credits and refunds—not 25% of the displayed sale price. The Partner Portal reconciliation is authoritative.

Holds and payout

30 days for Stripe and 60 days for AppSumo. Each ambassador must reach 100.00 in approved, unreversed commission in one currency. Payouts are reviewed and paid manually; no automatic transfer is promised.

Financial trust

Evidence first. No return claims.

  • Disclose the commission relationship with the recommendation itself.
  • Describe StockPortfolio.pro as research software, not personal investment advice.
  • Never promise returns, investment performance, data perfection or business longevity.
  • Check AI output against its cited filing before presenting it as fact.
  • No spam, self-referrals, impersonation, hidden incentives or unauthorized discounts.
  • Commissions are never offered for reviews. Reviews must remain honest and unincentivized.
