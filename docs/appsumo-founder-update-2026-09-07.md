Hey everyone — quick one from the founder's chair.

The fund pages got rebuilt this week, and it's the biggest data upgrade since launch. Everything below is live now on every AppSumo tier — pull up any fund you own and scroll.

**Every fund now shows its complete portfolio**
Not the 10-holding sample every fund page used to settle for — the whole thing. VOO: all 520 holdings. VTSAX: 3,546. BND: 17,409. Weight, market value and share count for each, and the list ends where the portfolio ends.

**Expense ratios you can trust**
Rebuilding the fee data turned up something worth sharing: the figures most finance sites quote are badly wrong for many mutual funds. Schwab's S&P 500 index fund is reported at 1.24%. Its own filing says **0.02%** — sixty times less. Where no fee table exists, we show nothing rather than a number that might be that far off.

**Bond funds got their missing half**
A bond fund has no sectors, so its page had a hole where equity funds show sector exposure. It now shows credit quality instead — the same question, asked the right way for bonds.

**Fund pages, generally**
Charts reach back further (1 month → Max), the AI summary reads the same portfolio the table shows, funds actually turn up in search, and a five-day-old NAV is no longer labelled "today".

**Your Usage page, rebuilt**
Remaining credits as the headline, then where they went, where they came from, and how fast you're burning through them — full ledger included.

**Smaller things this week**
- Public filing-change pages for any company — no account needed
- Market-cap bands in the screener, one toggle
- Ask no longer cites a stale annual filing as the latest one
- A page no longer goes blank when a data source misbehaves, and a price too old to trust is hidden instead of shown

Two reviews so far. If you've spent real time in the product, an honest one helps a great deal — positive, mixed or critical. No rating requested, nothing offered in return. It lives at appsumo.com/account/products.
