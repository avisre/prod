## Why reframe

The listing currently reads as a portfolio-tracking / stock-research tool that
happens to have AI in it. Buyers browsing AppSumo's AI category never see it, and
buyers who do see it compare it against portfolio trackers — a category where the
honest answer is that cheaper tools exist.

The product's actual differentiator is narrow and AI-shaped: **an AI that answers
financial questions and shows you the SEC filing each number came from.** That is
a claim almost no AI tool on the marketplace can make, and it is the frame the
listing should lead with.

The reframe is positioning, not a new claim. Nothing below describes a feature
that does not already ship.

> Ask any question about a US-listed company and get an answer with the SEC
> filing it came from, linked. No estimates, no hallucinated numbers.

## Opening paragraph (the reframe does its work here)

> Most AI finance tools give you a confident number and no way to check it.
> That's the problem this was built for.
>
> StockPortfolio.pro is an AI research assistant for US-listed companies that
> answers in plain English and links the SEC filing behind every figure it
> quotes. Ask "did diluted shares rise despite the buyback last quarter?" and you
> get the answer, the fiscal period, and a link straight to the 10-Q. If the
> number isn't in a filing, the answer says so instead of estimating it.

---

## The three feature blocks, in AI-first order

**1. Ask — cited answers, not confident guesses**
> Natural-language questions about any covered US company, answered from the
> filed document. Every figure keeps its fiscal period and its SEC source link.
> When a figure hasn't been filed, it comes back missing rather than invented.

**2. Filing Change Monitor — the AI reads the new filing for you**
> When a 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K lands, it's read against the previous one and you get
> what actually changed — risk-factor edits, language changes, the numbers that
> moved — ranked by materiality, each item linked to the filing. It replaces the
> manual first pass through a new filing.

**3. Filed-only data underneath**
> Up to 19 years of statements for covered US-listed companies that report in
> USD, straight from SEC filings. This is the layer that makes the AI checkable:
> the model isn't recalling numbers, it's reading filed ones.

---

## What to say about limits (keep this — it converts)

> Scope is deliberate: US-listed companies that report in USD to the SEC. No
> international coverage, no analyst estimates, no forward data, no price
> targets. If you want a market terminal, this isn't one.
