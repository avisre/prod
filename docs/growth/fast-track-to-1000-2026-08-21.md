## 2. The three levers, in order of speed

### Lever 1 — Re-sell the 9 existing buyers (48 hours, $0)
AppSumo explicitly supports this: buyers can buy again / upgrade a license as
long as the deal hasn't sold out (stacking = buying more codes; licensing =
upgrade/downgrade). You have 9 people who already trusted you once. That is the
cheapest money available.

**Action:** one email, no discount, deadline honesty:
"Your Tier 1/2 includes N Ask queries/mo. Tier 3 gives you the full stack.
You can upgrade before the deal closes [date]. No promo — just heads-up."

- 6 of 9 upgrading ≈ $359 net. Zero marketing, one subject line.
- **Pre-check (Day 0):** your listing's Deal Terms and version mapping must
  actually allow upgrade/stack. You know the version-mapping is fragile — if
  uncertain, one line to your AppSumo manager before sending.

### Lever 2 — Switch on AppSumo's affiliate network (day 1)
When an affiliate sells your deal, AppSumo pays the affiliate AND you keep your
90–95% share — the affiliate is paid on top, not out of your cut. New buyers via
affiliates are your highest-margin orders by far.

**Action:** make sure the listing's affiliate program is enabled in the Partner
Portal, then ask 3–5 people you trust (or small AppSumo-adjacent accounts) to
share their affiliate link. Each affiliate selling 1 Tier 3 = ~$135 net to you,
every time.

### Lever 3 — One review week (day 2–3)
"Top 5 Trending" (emails Tue–Fri) and the dedicated Wed/Fri campaign emails are
selection made from **previous-day sales + reviews + taco ratings**. One week of
concentrated reviews (from the 6 buyers who've had a useful Ask, no incentive —
FTC-clean) turns on AppSumo's ~1.3M-list amplification. This is the *free* boost
the marketplace gives a product that performs.

## 4. Kill rules / pivots (no infinite marketing)

- **If the 9-buyer email gets ≤1 reply in 48h:** the 70% reorder lever is cold.
  Drop it, double the affiliate ask (10 sharers), move.
- **If zero reviews land in 5 days:** no Top-5 Trending. Pivot: the Desk
  single-order path (1 × $1,961 ≈ clears the whole goal in one direct
  conversation, off-AppSumo). It's a sales call, not marketing.
- **First new-customer order attributes below 90%:** re-model immediately —
  the share assumption is the plan's biggest unknown.

## 5. What does NOT change (from the flywheel plan, verbatim)

- No API/MCP, no TradingView import, no pricing change, no new tier, no versions
  edit in the Partner Portal.
- No paid acquisition until refunds/payouts/AI cost are mature.

  cite-a-source meaning.
