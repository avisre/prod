The detailed NVDA source-backed original generated 76 impressions, 23 engagements and seven link clicks. The 37 classified replies generated two link clicks in total. One dilution reply generated 355 impressions and one click; a filing-timeline reply generated 21 impressions and one click.

## Classification

- **Vanity/reach posts:** high impressions or engagement without a site action.
- **Traffic posts:** the detailed NVDA original is the clearest example.
- **Research-activation posts:** not proven because post-level site completion joins are incomplete.
- **Revenue posts:** none verified.

First-party events attributed to X include 18 AppSumo outbound actions across eight identified actors, but zero verified signups, activations or purchases. Ten X-attributed events have no durable identity. Do not call X a revenue channel.

## What to do with X

Use fewer, stronger original research posts built around one surprising but verifiable question:

1. headline claim;
2. filing-derived number and period;
3. why the number can mislead without context;
4. visible primary-source citation;
5. one CTA to reproduce the research.

Replies should be reserved for genuine investor questions where the product produces a directly relevant answer. The objective is a completed research workflow, not reply count.

## Seven-day test

Publish one high-evidence original per day and no more than five genuinely relevant replies. Give each link a unique content/click ID. Success is at least 20 qualified site landings, five completed research actions and one attributable order. Stop scaling if 20 qualified landings produce zero research completions, or if seven days produce zero identifiable movement beyond clicks.

## Why this might be wrong

- Native export and manual tracker cover different row sets.
- Link clicks may include repeats or owner QA.
- Missing identity can hide assisted purchases.
- One NVDA post is not enough to generalize across tickers or formats.
