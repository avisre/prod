## Q: "Is this as good as Koyfin?"

**Short answer: no — and here's who should buy Koyfin instead.**

They're different tools, so the honest answer is that Koyfin wins outright at
the job it's built for.

Koyfin is a market-data and charting terminal. It covers global equities, ETFs,
FX and macro series, its charting and dashboards are far deeper than ours, and
it carries analyst estimates. It's also a much more mature product than we are.

**Buy Koyfin instead of us if:**

- you want a terminal open all day — quotes, charts, watchlists, macro dashboards
- you invest outside the US, or in companies that don't report in USD (we don't
  cover those names at all)
- analyst estimates or consensus are part of your workflow
- charting depth is what you'd actually use most days

StockPortfolio.pro does one narrower job: reading SEC filings for US-listed
companies. Two things it does that a charting terminal doesn't:

1. **Citation-grounded Ask** — ask a question in English and the answer comes
   back with the SEC filing it was drawn from, linked, so you can open the
   primary document and check it. When a figure can't be grounded in a filing,
   the answer says so instead of estimating.
2. **Filing diffs** — we read the newest 10-K/10-Q/8-K against the previous one
   and show what changed, ranked by materiality, each item linked back to the
   filed document.

Plenty of people should honestly run both: Koyfin for market monitoring and
non-US coverage, us for the moment a filing lands.

The full version, including where we're weaker, is here:
https://www.stockportfolio.pro/compare/vs-koyfin

## Q: "How does this compare to Seeking Alpha?"

Seeking Alpha is a research-and-opinion publisher with a large contributor
community and a ratings layer on top. We publish no opinion and no ratings at
all — we read filings and answer questions about them with the filing linked.

If you want other investors' theses, a rating to react to, or earnings-call
coverage, that's Seeking Alpha, not us.

Our comparison page is deliberately short because we won't publish feature
claims about another company we haven't verified:
https://www.stockportfolio.pro/compare/vs-seeking-alpha
