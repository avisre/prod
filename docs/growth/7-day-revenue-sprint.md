# Seven-day revenue sprint

Goal: validate and begin the shortest founder-proceeds path without building a new feature. Primary score is verified orders and partner/direct proceeds, not impressions.

## Day 1 — establish the selling truth

- Capture the current AppSumo baseline: 122 seven-day visitors, 1.64% conversion, nine total orders, $801 gross, $183.97 partner revenue, zero refunds.
- Verify the unique partner link and its written 90% new-customer condition; do not assume existing AppSumo users qualify.
- QA one complete flow: question → cited answer → source opened → Tier 3 checkout link.
- Select one real company question that demonstrates a non-obvious filing insight without making an investment recommendation.
- Prepare one screenshot/demo and a one-sentence limitation.

Output: one authoritative proof asset, one link, one baseline.

## Day 2 — recover existing value

- Offer founder help to the old unredeemed Tier 3 buyer once; give the new Tier 2 buyer normal activation time.
- Review the five active lower-tier buyers’ observed usage before proposing any upgrade explanation.
- Help each contacted user answer one research question first. Only then explain Tier 3 capacity if relevant.

Output: activation outcome and evidence; do not count an email as progress.

## Day 3 — high-intent marketplace conversion

- Ensure current AppSumo Q&A has precise, non-promissory answers on ETF coverage, exports, alerts, API/MCP and plan mapping.
- Prepare the listing clarification/proof change for explicit approval; do not change the public listing in this analysis phase.
- Place the proof workflow where an active investment-research discussion already exists; use the unique link only when it helps answer the question.

Output: qualified partner-link visits and orders.

## Day 4 — three partner demonstrations

- Contact only three source-oriented analysts/newsletters with a current filing-based trigger.
- Research one ticker for each, show the exact filing evidence and ask whether it saved diligence time.
- No bulk creator pitch, sponsorship claim or generic demo.

Output: replies, demonstrations booked and attributable visits.

## Day 5 — professional price test

- Conduct up to five conversations from the qualified Desk cohort.
- Show the current product; ask about workflow, required provenance, frequency and budget.
- Offer Desk only if the existing product solves the job. Do not promise API/MCP or bespoke compliance features.

Output: demo/budget evidence, not feature requests alone.

## Day 6 — remove the biggest observed objection

- Review AppSumo visitor/order movement and sampled Clarity sessions.
- Choose one issue only: unclear evidence, tier confusion, source-link friction or activation reliability.
- Prepare the smallest corrective copy/UX change for approval. Do not change pricing or build a feature.

Output: one bounded conversion fix with rollback metric.

## Day 7 — revenue decision

Report:

- qualified partner-link visits;
- new-to-AppSumo eligibility;
- orders by tier;
- gross GMV, discounts and partner proceeds;
- redemptions;
- first useful Ask results;
- repeat users;
- refunds;
- professional demos and budget discussions.

Decision rules:

- Partner link pays 90% and converts: continue until seven eligible Tier 3 buyers.
- Clicks but no orders: fix trust/listing/offer, not traffic volume.
- Orders but no redemption: fix activation.
- Redemption but no useful Ask: fix product reliability/onboarding.
- Ask success but no return: strengthen filing-change monitoring.
- No professional budget signal after 15 qualified conversations: stop Desk-first work.
- Refunds exceed 10%: pause acquisition and diagnose fit/expectations.

## Daily guardrails

- No fake urgency, guaranteed returns or absolute accuracy claims.
- No cold bulk email, spray-and-pray replies or paid ads.
- One source-backed asset can be repurposed; do not create content merely to fill a cadence.
- Finance truth comes from AppSumo/Stripe, not redemptions or funnel labels.
- Direct/unknown remains unknown.
