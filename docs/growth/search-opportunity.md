### What is working

Comparison pages account for 16 of 19 full-period clicks and all five clicks in the latest 28 days. The two latest seven-day clicks were also comparisons. The only exposed clicked query/page pair was `mur vs lib` → `/compare/LB-vs-MUR`: one click, 23 impressions, 4.35% CTR and average position 6.96.

Protect the comparison family. Improve exact company/ticker naming, fresh filing periods and answer clarity only where current queries support it. Do not replace URLs, canonicals or the answer-first structure.

### What is not proven demand

WAB EPS/shares/dividend pages have large impression counts and zero clicks, dominated by quoted, test-like strings such as `"earnings per share - wab"`. Over the full period, WAB dividend and shares pages received 3,477 and 1,892 impressions respectively but no clicks. This is rank-check or anomalous demand until demonstrated otherwise, not a commercial keyword cluster.

Playtika–Yelp produced 979 impressions around position 10 and no clicks over the full period, then disappeared from the latest 28-day view. Treat it as a title/intent diagnostic, not a conversion opportunity.

## Bing

Bing reports 38 clicks and 3,929 impressions across 67 traffic days, 17,707 indexed pages, 24,575 sitemap URLs and zero active crawl issues. Query and page endpoints contain data through 14 August, but an export suitable for a clean conversion join was not produced during this audit. Bing is a real traffic source, but it has zero attributable purchases in current internal data.

## Highest-value search work

1. Preserve comparison pages, the only Google click-producing family.
2. Inspect the 20 Bing pages/queries with the most real clicks before changing shared templates.
3. Improve answer quality, source context and internal next steps on already-clicked pages; no new programmatic page set.
4. Separate human-looking queries from quoted/rank-check patterns.
5. Measure organic landing → meaningful research → signup → order; current search systems stop at clicks.

Search is a compounding channel, not the shortest route to the next $816 in founder proceeds. It should not displace high-intent marketplace conversion work this week.

## Why this might be wrong

- Search Console hides low-volume queries and cannot identify buyers.
- Bing page/query conversion data is incomplete.
- Google recrawl delays mean recent SEO changes have not reached a clean post-period.
- Comparison clicks may be curiosity rather than buying intent.
