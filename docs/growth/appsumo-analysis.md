## Buyer signal

The one public review calls the product “very efficient” while saying the reviewer still needs to cross-verify data accuracy. This is excellent evidence for the job “research faster with evidence available,” but it is not a blanket accuracy endorsement.

Questions reveal five concrete anxieties:

1. whether ETFs are covered;
2. how AppSumo tiers map to normal plans;
3. whether Ask is reliable;
4. whether reports can be exported;
5. whether API/MCP and compound smart alerts exist.

The API/MCP/alert request came from one technically oriented prospect who said fundamentals would be a secondary confluence to technical analysis. It is a strong discovery interview, not market validation for an API build.

One presale buyer explicitly sought Tier 3 and even offered to pay directly so more money would reach the product. That is a high-confidence trust/value signal for the top AppSumo tier.

## Listing conversion

The current seven-day card shows 122 unique visitors and a 1.64% conversion rate, consistent with two orders and 120 nonbuyers. That is the largest measured leak.

The listing should be evaluated around four questions before adding more features:

- Can a buyer see one complete question → answer → cited filing workflow?
- Are ETF, export and alert capabilities described precisely rather than implied broadly?
- Is the difference between 30/100/300 Ask limits immediately clear?
- Are absolute AI/data claims replaced with inspectable evidence and limits?

No edit is made in this audit.

## Economics

The average partner revenue is $20.44 per current order. Tier 3 partner revenue averaged $34.57 across its three orders. At those observed economics:

- Two more Tier 3 orders close the $199 gross-GMV gap and take booked gross to $1,099.
- Twenty-four more Tier 3 orders close the $816.03 founder-revenue gap before future refunds.
- Forty more orders at the current blended payout close the founder-revenue gap.

The promotion screen advertises a 90% share for **new customers brought to AppSumo** through the unique partner URL. Seven new-to-AppSumo Tier 3 orders at $149 and 90% would contribute up to $938.70 before discounts/refunds and cross the founder-revenue target. This is a promising hypothesis, not observed unit economics: all nine current buyers were already AppSumo users.

## Why this might be wrong

- AppSumo buyers are an LTD-selected cohort and may not resemble subscribers.
- Partner revenue may still be pending settlement even with no current refund.
- One review and a few Q&A items are tiny samples.
- The 90% partner-link rate may have eligibility, discount or attribution conditions not visible in the headline.
