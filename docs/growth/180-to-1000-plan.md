# Four explicit paths from the current baseline

The primary recommendation remains the **FASTEST PLAUSIBLE** route below: validate one new-to-AppSumo order through the unique partner link, then pursue seven Tier 3 purchases only if the advertised economics appear in the Partner Portal. It is the shortest evidence-constrained route, not a forecast. The other scenarios make the trade-off between probability, founder effort and upside explicit.

## FASTEST PLAUSIBLE — validate, then scale the 90% partner-link route

**WHO:** Serious self-directed US-equity investors who are new to AppSumo and already perform filing-based diligence.

**WHAT:** The existing AppSumo Pro/Tier 3 LTD—300 monthly Ask questions, research workflows and evidence attached—through the unique partner link. No new feature or tier.

**PRICE:** $149 once. The proceeds model uses an illustrative 10% buyer discount and the advertised 90% new-customer partner share.

**NUMBER REQUIRED:** Two eligible Tier 3 purchases clear the separate $1,000 gross-booked-sales target. Seven eligible Tier 3 purchases are required to take modeled cumulative founder proceeds above $1,000.

**CHANNEL:** Source-oriented investor and newsletter audiences, high-intent company-research conversations, the qualified analyst/investor prospect list and one repeatable cited-workflow demo. X is a feeder only because it has generated clicks but no verified buyer.

**WHY NOW:** The offer, proof workflow and partner link already exist; six of seven redeemed buyers used Ask, all four Tier 2/3 buyers did, and one buyer explicitly sought Tier 3 after seeing the LTD. This tests distribution and partner economics without product work.

**EXPECTED CONVERSION:** The only observed benchmark is 1.64% listing visitor-to-order over the latest seven-day card. At that rate, seven orders require roughly 427 qualified landings, rounded to a 430-landing operating requirement. This is not a validated conversion rate for new-to-AppSumo partner-link traffic or for Tier 3 specifically.

**EVIDENCE:** The listing has already produced nine real orders; the best sourced X original produced seven clicks while mass replies produced little click traffic; provenance and visible filing context match the usage of retained buyers. Generic financial AI/API/MCP positioning is crowded, so the narrower evidence-inspection workflow is the defensible message.

**DEPENDENCIES:** The prospect must be a qualifying new AppSumo customer; attribution must survive the partner-link window; the advertised 90% rate must apply; discounts and refunds must remain within the model; Ask reliability, source links and existing export/alert claims must hold. The first attributed order is the economics test.

**TIME TO CLOSE:** 14–28 days only if the campaign can generate about 430 qualified partner-link landings. AppSumo cash arrives later under its refund and Net-60 settlement cycle.

**GROSS AND FOUNDER PROCEEDS:** Seven purchases create $1,043 additional sticker-price GMV and $1,844 cumulative AppSumo sticker-price GMV. At `$149 × 90% discounted price × 90% partner share`, modeled incremental founder proceeds are $844.83 and modeled cumulative partner revenue is $1,028.80. These are conditional proceeds, not cash already received.

**FAILURE / KILL:** Re-model immediately if the first correctly attributed new-customer order does not receive the advertised economics. Stop the message/landing combination after 100 qualified partner-link visits with zero orders. Do not scale if buyers prove to be existing AppSumo customers, attribution is unreliable or early refunds invalidate the unit economics.

## MOST LIKELY — continue with observed AppSumo marketplace economics

**WHO:** Existing AppSumo customers who research US equities and value sourced Ask, screeners, comparisons and portfolio tracking.

**WHAT:** Keep the current three-tier lifetime offer unchanged. For a proceeds-first variant, emphasize Tier 3 without promising that every buyer will choose it; for the base case, retain the observed blended tier mix.

**PRICE:** $39 Starter, $79 Investor and $149 Pro/Tier 3, paid once.

**NUMBER REQUIRED:** Six Starter, three Investor or two Pro purchases clear $1,000 gross booked sales from the $801 AppSumo baseline. Founder proceeds require either 24 additional Tier 3 buyers at the observed $34.57 Tier 3 payout or approximately 40 additional buyers at the observed $20.441 blended payout per order.

**CHANNEL:** The live AppSumo listing, current website bridge, approved listing/Q&A improvements, buyer activation and evidence-led organic distribution. No paid acquisition until mature refunds, revenue share and lifetime support/AI cost are known.

**WHY NOW:** This is the only channel with verified orders and partner revenue. It does not depend on unvalidated new-customer partner economics or a new direct-sales buyer type.

**EXPECTED CONVERSION:** Use 1.64% listing visitor-to-any-order as the current observed benchmark, not a Tier 3 forecast. Holding that rate constant implies about 1,464 landings for 24 orders or 2,439 for 40 orders. The actual requirement changes with tier mix, listing traffic quality and refunds.

**EVIDENCE:** Nine real AppSumo orders generated $183.97 displayed partner revenue; the observed Tier 3 payout averages $34.57 and the all-order blended payout averages $20.441. Six of seven redeemed buyers have used Ask. The Partner Portal, not redemptions or outbound clicks, remains the commercial source of truth.

**DEPENDENCIES:** Listing traffic must persist; the historical payout and tier mix must remain relevant; refunds, support load and lifetime AI cost must stay tolerable; activation must continue to produce useful research outcomes.

**TIME TO CLOSE:** At the current card's roughly two orders per seven days, about 12 weeks for 24 orders or 20 weeks for 40 orders. More traffic or higher conversion could shorten this, but the prior 6–12 week aspiration is not supported for the 40-order blended case at the current observed rate. Net-60 settlement makes cash receipt later than order capture.

**GROSS AND FOUNDER PROCEEDS:** Twenty-four Tier 3 purchases create $3,576 additional sticker-price GMV and $4,377 cumulative AppSumo sticker-price GMV; modeled cumulative founder revenue is $1,013.65. Forty buyers at the historical $89 average sticker price create about $3,560 additional sticker-price GMV and $4,361 cumulative sticker-price GMV; modeled cumulative founder revenue is about $1,001.61. Neither figure reserves for future refunds.

**FAILURE / KILL:** Pause acquisition if refunds exceed 10% or two activation failures occur in one week. Change the message or listing after 100 qualified external clicks with fewer than five purchases. Re-model if payout per order, tier mix or support/AI burden deteriorates materially.

## LOWEST EFFORT — close two existing Power Annual checkouts

Here, "lowest effort" means lowest product and operational change, not highest probability: the published self-serve plan and checkout already exist, but direct demand is unvalidated.

**WHO:** Serious individual investors who need Filing Change Monitor, a filing feed and cross-account wash-sale tooling but do not need client-facing commercial-use permission.

**WHAT:** Existing Power Annual subscription with everything in Pro plus the professional monitoring workflow. No custom contract, new feature or new package.

**PRICE:** $579 per year, billed upfront.

**NUMBER REQUIRED:** One purchase clears $1,000 gross booked sales from the $801 AppSumo baseline. Two retained purchases are required to close the $816.03 founder-proceeds gap under the plan's before-cost arithmetic.

**CHANNEL:** Existing homepage and checkout, plus a small number of high-intent founder follow-ups using a prospect's ticker and current Filing Monitor workflow. Do not run broad outbound or build bespoke requests.

**WHY NOW:** Power is already published, self-serve and differentiated by a live monitoring job. Two retained transactions can reach the proceeds target without the order volume or Net-60 delay of normal AppSumo economics.

**EXPECTED CONVERSION:** Unknown: there is no active MRR and the only historical Stripe payment was refunded. A two-from-20 qualified-conversation hurdle (10%) is a planning test, not an evidence-backed forecast.

**EVIDENCE:** The live offer and checkout exist, and Filing Change Monitor is the clearest Power differentiator. Against that, there are no retained direct customers, so willingness to pay at $579 is not validated.

**DEPENDENCIES:** Stripe checkout must work; the buyer must value monitoring enough to prepay annually; the published product claims must be reliable; purchases must survive the seven-day refund window. Actual Stripe fees, tax, FX and affiliate deductions must be taken from balance transactions rather than assumed away.

**TIME TO CLOSE:** Plausibly 2–8 weeks for two retained self-serve or founder-assisted purchases, plus seven-day refund clearance. There is insufficient evidence for a narrower forecast.

**GROSS AND FOUNDER PROCEEDS:** Two purchases create $1,158 direct gross and $1,959 combined gross when added to current AppSumo sticker GMV. Before costs, cumulative founder-revenue signal is $1,341.97. At an explicitly illustrative 95% direct-net realization, retained direct proceeds would be $1,100.10 and cumulative provisional founder proceeds would be $1,284.07.

**FAILURE / KILL:** Stop treating this as low-effort after 20 qualified conversations without two retained purchases, or earlier if 15 conversations produce no serious demo or budget discussion. Do not add features to rescue an unvalidated price point.

## 30-day operating plan

- Run the new-to-AppSumo partner-link test and validate its first order economics.
- Convert warm, explicit research intent with one source-backed workflow.
- Resolve old unredeemed activation and answer open Q&A precisely.
- Use the current offer; no pricing or feature expansion.

### Next: activation and conversion, days 8–21

- Make first useful cited Ask the onboarding target.
- Separate users who complete one Ask from those who return to another company/filing.
- Improve only the listing objection shown by recordings/Q&A.
- Run ten professional Desk discovery conversations without coding to requests.

### Later: moat and compounding distribution, days 22–30+

- Turn reliable filing changes into a return loop.
- Protect comparison pages, the only proven Google click family.
- Repair order/attribution measurement in a separately approved implementation.
- Build API/MCP only after at least three priced commitments and verified upstream commercial rights.

## What not to do

- Do not build 30 more tools or broad SEO pages for immediate revenue.
- Do not treat X replies, impressions or AppSumo redemptions as sales.
- Do not add API/MCP from one request.
- Do not discount below the current LTD without unit economics.
- Do not promise that AI cannot hallucinate or that every displayed number is directly filed.
- Do not invite the two unredeemed buyers into referral programs before they receive value.
