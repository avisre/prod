## Short description (~150 words)

Anyone can get a chatbot with verified answers — that's a commodity now. What
StockPortfolio.pro builds is the report itself. A Research Dossier is an
initiation-grade write-up of a US-listed company, generated from its SEC
filings: the same sections, in the same order, for every company, with every
figure cited to the filing it came from — so companies are comparable side by
side instead of comparable only in a chat window. The Filing Change Monitor then
watches your companies and digests each new 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K into plain
English, ranked by materiality, so you know what changed without the manual
first pass. A sourced AI analyst with a monthly question allowance, free
screeners, 19-year statement history and mixed stocks/ETFs/mutual-fund
portfolios are included. One-time purchase, three tiers.

## Long description

Investing research has three jobs: understand a business, compare it with the
alternatives, and keep your understanding current as it files. StockPortfolio.pro
is built around those three jobs rather than around a chat window.

**1. Build the Dossier.** Type a ticker — no prompting. You get the
initiation-grade report: a decision brief in plain English, the financial
trajectory computed from up to 19 years of filed statements, business segments,
what's priced in, the bull and the bear case, key risks, and what just changed
in the latest filing. Because every dossier answers the same questions in the
same order, companies are genuinely comparable: a comparison view lines up two
or three of them side by side. Every figure links to the filing it came from —
if the evidence does not support a claim, the product says so.

**2. Watch the filings.** Add your companies to the Filing Change Monitor. When
a new 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K lands, it reads the filing and tells you in plain
English what changed and how much it matters, ranked by materiality — the
manual first pass through a new filing, done.

**3. Ask the AI analyst.** For everything in between, ask directly: company
comparisons, fund costs and holdings, trend questions. Company figures come
from supported filings; fund facts from labelled fund data; anything after the
latest filing from dated live-web sources, identified separately.

Also included: stock screeners (17 + custom filters), side-by-side company
compare, portfolio tracking for stocks, ETFs and mutual funds together, and a
browsable public research library. No brokerage credentials required.

Plans: lifetime access from $39 (one time). Tiers differ by watchlist size and
Ask allowance (see tier table).
