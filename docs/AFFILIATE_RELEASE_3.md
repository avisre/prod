# Release 3 — professional partners and automation (design only)

Release 3 is gated. Do not implement or enable it until at least 10 attributable
direct purchases exist, five partners have generated a purchase, refund
performance is acceptable and commission calculations have been manually
verified.

The future scope is professional lead attribution, Desk/Firm opportunity
tracking, statements, payout-provider integration, multiple campaigns, partner
performance reports and stronger fraud scoring. It is intentionally not part of
the customer ambassador MVP.
