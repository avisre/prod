## Long description ("From the founding team")

**Why we built this.** We ran a simple test with our own lifetime customers: we
asked them what would make them use the product every week. The answer that
changed our roadmap came from a customer who has been with us since our first
AppSumo deal — and it wasn't a new feature, it was a correction:

> "Anyone can get a chatbot with verified answers with web search and SEC EDGAR
> data." *(AppSumo buyer, August 2026)*

They were right. A chat is a commodity. What isn't is the thing the chat was
pretending to be: the research itself — written down, in a consistent
professional structure, with sources. So we stopped shipping chat-first features
and shipped the missing workflow.

**Two institutional habits, made one-click:**

**1. The Dossier. Click the ticker, read the report.**
An institutional desk starts a company with an initiation report: business,
trajectory, segments, valuation, cases, risks. We generate exactly that from
the company's SEC filings — same sections, same order, every company, every
figure linked to the filing it came from. You never open a prompt box; the
questions are already asked, and they're an analyst's questions.

**Compare rides on structure, not effort.**
Because every dossier is built the same way, two or three line up side by side
in one click — aligned growth, margins, what keeps the money, what's priced
in, cases and risks. Five credits, one decision.

**2. The Monitor. Every filing on your watchlist, read for you.**
The dossier earns your research each time you use it; the monitor keeps it
current. Add your companies once: each new 10-K, 10-Q or 8-K is read and comes
back as plain English — what changed, how material it is, a link into the
filing. Quarterly updates stop being a weekend of PDFs.

**Everything in between, kept in its place.**
When something still needs chasing — fund holdings, trends — a sourced AI
analyst answers, its source class shown. Plus 17 screeners, up to 19 years of
filed statements, and portfolios that mix stocks, ETFs and mutual funds.

**What it's not** (from the founding team, on purpose): no trade execution, no
brokerage aggregation, no tick-by-tick terminal, no advice. Filings land
quarterly — the value is the digest, not millisecond latency.

**Also included:** 17 screeners plus custom filters, up to 19 years of filed
statements (48 quarters), portfolios that mix stocks, ETFs and mutual funds, a
public research library, and no brokerage credentials — ever.

**Pricing:** lifetime access from **$39 one time**. Three tiers differ by
watchlist size and monthly question allowance (see table).

## FAQ

**How is this different from asking ChatGPT/Claude about a stock?**
A chat produces prose you have to verify line by line; the questions are
yours to write and repeat for every company. A dossier is written research:
same structure, verified sources, aligned across companies — and a monitor so
it stays current. You use the AI analyst for the loose ends, not the foundation.

**Where do the numbers come from?**
The company's SEC filings. Each figure in a dossier links to the filing it
came from; the analyst's answers are labelled by source class. If the filings
don't support a claim, the product says so.

**Do I need to connect a brokerage account?**
No. Nothing in the product needs broker credentials.

**How recent is the research?**
Dossiers build from the latest filed statements (up to 19 years of history) and
the Monitor digests each new filing as it lands — including 8-Ks.

**Is there a monthly fee?**
No. The AppSumo deal is one-time for lifetime access. Tiers differ by
watchlist size and monthly analyst questions.

**What happens if a company I follow isn't covered?**
6,000+ US stocks are covered today; anything missing reports back honestly
instead of inventing a report.
