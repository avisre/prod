# Release 2 — external affiliates (design only)

Release 2 is gated. Do not implement or enable it until at least three customer
ambassadors are active, five attributable purchases exist (or the owner gives an
explicit override), customer feedback is recorded, and production attribution
has been verified.

The future scope is an application and approval workflow, external dashboard,
deep-link generator, creative library, optional 60-day Power access, campaign
tracking, fraud flags and support-only email templates. Recruitment is planned
at roughly 40 investment newsletters, 30 fundamental-investing YouTubers, 15
finance educators/communities, 10 X/LinkedIn creators and 5 RIA/adviser-focused
creators. No public application, free access or external payout exists in
Release 1.
