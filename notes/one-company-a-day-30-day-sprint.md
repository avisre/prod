# StockPortfolio.pro — One-Company-a-Day 30-Day Research Sprint

Status: ready for execution. This document adds one daily original-company deep dive to the existing high-intent X reply program; it does not replace the reply strategy.

## Operating rule

For 30 consecutive publishing days, choose one company and answer one falsifiable research question. Publish the same research across:

1. A source-linked StockPortfolio.pro research page.
2. An 8–12-post X thread.
3. A 700–1,200-word LinkedIn note.
4. Four to six evidence-matching charts or screenshots.

The commercial CTA appears only after the analysis has delivered value. Use `source=x` or `source=linkedin` and a stable `content_id`; never invent an AppSumo deadline.

Continue separately with 15–20 useful, high-intent X replies per day. Replies may cover any company and should answer the question first; the daily deep-dive company controls original content only.

## Publish gate

Do not publish until all checks pass:

- Candidate scores at least 7/10: freshness 0–3, investor interest 0–3, research opportunity 0–4.
- One central question is stated and is falsifiable.
- At least three findings are non-obvious, material, and tied to a filing or first-party data.
- Five-year financial trend is checked where data is available.
- Cash flow, capex, working capital, SBC/dilution, balance sheet, filing changes, and valuation are considered when relevant.
- One next-quarter number to watch is named with a reason.
- Every image proves the adjacent written claim.
- Missing or incomparable periods are disclosed.
- No generic AI filler, investment prediction, guaranteed return, or unsupported conclusion remains.
- Source links point to SEC filings or StockPortfolio.pro first-party data.
- The CTA is tracked and points to the relevant research/tool workflow.

If three meaningful findings cannot be supported, change the company rather than manufacturing insight.

## Daily workflow

1. Score two or three candidates; select the highest-quality fresh opportunity.
2. State the central question before opening the draft.
3. Understand business economics and segment/geographic/customer drivers.
4. Compare five-year revenue, profit, operating cash flow, free cash flow, capex, and shares where available.
5. Explain revenue quality and the margin bridge rather than repeating headline growth.
6. Investigate what created cash: working capital, non-cash items, SBC, and capex.
7. Review shareholder economics: SBC, buybacks, dividends, and net share-count change.
8. Review balance-sheet capacity and new or changed filing risks.
9. Compare management guidance with subsequent outcomes when comparable data exists.
10. Frame valuation as the operational expectations embedded in the price.
11. Select four to six matching visuals and write captions that state exactly what each proves.
12. Run the publish gate, then create the X thread, LinkedIn note, research page, and tracked CTA.
13. Record content, intent, product-value, and conversion metrics after publication.

## X thread structure

1. Central question and why it matters.
2. Five-year trend and implication.
3. Finding one: observation → evidence → why it matters.
4. Finding two: revenue quality, margins, segment economics, or guidance.
5. Cash earnings quality, working capital, and capex.
6. SBC, buybacks, dilution, or dividends.
7. Balance sheet and capital allocation.
8. Valuation expectations.
9. Evidence-backed bull case.
10. Evidence-backed bear case.
11. The one or two variables to watch next.
12. Conclusion, source-linked StockPortfolio.pro workflow, tracked AppSumo CTA, and disclaimer.

Use fewer posts when the evidence is complete. Do not add length for its own sake.

## LinkedIn structure

Use the same company and question. Explain the three to five strongest findings in 700–1,200 words, include matching visuals, a short bull/bear section, the next-quarter variable, source links, the StockPortfolio.pro workflow, tracked research link, and “Not investment advice.”

## Measurement

Record per company: X impressions, engagement, profile visits, link clicks, LinkedIn impressions, company/research-page visits, tool views and completions, source opens, AppSumo outbound clicks, signups, trials, meaningful activations, redemptions, and attributable/probable customers.

Review intent and conversion after 7–10 days. Optimize the research angle—not the number of posts—based on activated customers and qualified tool use.

## Safety and scope

- No automatic posting, replies, DMs, likes, follows, or fabricated engagement.
- No cold customer email outside the approved support workflow and cooldown.
- No new programmatic SEO page set until existing tool and research demand is measured.
- No claim of investment advice, prediction, guaranteed accuracy, or guaranteed returns.
- Do not store prompts, passwords, cookies, reset links, or service credentials in the tracker.
